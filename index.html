How to add it to Gmail
  1. Click Copy signature for Gmail above.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings (gear icon) → See all settings → General.
  3. Under Signature, click Create new (or select your existing one) and paste into the editor box.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

For other email clients (Outlook, Apple Mail, etc.) use Download .html or Copy HTML code.
